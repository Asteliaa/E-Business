[6]: For Flipbook feature restrictions, please see the Flipbook Maker section on this page. Contact Us Frequently Asked Question What is Visual Paradigm Online? Visual Paradigm Online (VP Online) is a web-based, unified platform offering a wide array of AI-powered applications and tools. It caters to diverse needs including diagramming, graphic design, strategic analysis, documentation, knowledge management, animation, flipbook creation, and PDF editing. What is the difference between Visual Paradigm Online and Visual Paradigm Desktop? Visual Paradigm Online is a comprehensive, web-based platform with numerous applications and tools. In contrast, Visual Paradigm Desktop is a dedicated desktop software focused on visual modeling, business analysis, DevOps, and enterprise architecture. Notably, users with an active Visual Paradigm Desktop license receive complimentary access to VP Online's corresponding tools and features (e.g., Desktop Professional Edition users gain access to VP Online Combo Edition features). Does your product support AI? Yes, we extensively integrate AI to enhance value for our customers! Many of our features and applications are AI-ready. For instance, our AI Chatbot rapidly generates diagrams and answers questions. VPasCode leverages AI to correct PlantUML and Mermaid code, while OpenDocs uses AI to generate and embed diagrams directly into your pages. Our commitment to this technology is broad, and we will continue implementing AI across our products. Do I need to pay additional to use AI? No. AI capabilities are included with your subscription. As long as you have an active subscription, you can fully utilize our AI features at no additional cost. Do I need to download or install anything to use Visual Paradigm Online? No. Visual Paradigm Online is entirely cloud-based. You can access it from any laptop or desktop computer with an internet connection, without needing to download or install anything. How do paid plans work? Payments are made automatically in US Dollars for your convenience. You can cancel your subscription at any time, and you will not be charged for the upcoming billing cycle. Which methods of payment do you accept? We accept major credit cards including Mastercard, Visa, Discover, and American Express. Additionally, you can pay using your PayPal account or via wire transfer. What happens to my content when I cancel my subscription? Upon cancellation, your account will revert to a free account with limited features. However, you will still be able to access all content you previously created. You can always upgrade again to regain access to premium features. What browsers do you support? We fully support modern versions of major browsers like Edge, Firefox, Chrome, and Opera across various operating systems. While Safari and certain older browsers may exhibit inconsistent behavior or limited functionality, Internet Explorer (IE) and Microsoft® Edge Legacy are not supported. For the best experience, we strongly recommend keeping your browser updated.
